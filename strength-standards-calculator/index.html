How these standards work

What each level means: Beginner = you can do the lift with good form. Novice = about 6–12 months of consistent training. Intermediate = 1–3 years. Advanced = roughly the average drug-free competitive powerlifter. Elite = roughly the top 10% of drug-free competitive powerlifters aged 18–35.

Why it isn't just "times bodyweight": strength doesn't grow in a straight line with bodyweight. A lifter twice as heavy is not twice as strong; strength scales closer to bodyweight to the power of 0.67. Simple bodyweight multiples make lighter lifters look stronger and heavier lifters look weaker, so these standards are scaled allometrically instead.

Age: relative strength peaks around 18–35 and declines gradually after that, so the standards are adjusted down for older age groups.
