The breakdown

TDEE = Total Daily Energy Expenditure

Where you actually burn calories in a day. It's made of 4 parts.

~70%

BMR

Basal metabolic rate

The calories your body burns at complete rest: keeping your heart beating, lungs breathing, brain thinking and temperature regulated, 24/7. It's about 70% of what you burn in a day, and it's the part that certain metabolic conditions can suppress (hypothyroidism can lower it by 5–15%).

Varies

NEAT

Non-exercise activity thermogenesis

Calories from any movement that isn't formal exercise: steps, fidgeting, standing instead of sitting. Depending on your lifestyle it's the smallest chunk or the second biggest, and it's one of the best levers for burning more in a day.

10–15%

TEF

Thermic effect of food

Calories burned digesting food. It depends on what you eat: protein costs 20–30% of its calories to digest, so 100 calories of protein burns 20–30 just breaking it down. Fat costs only 0–3%.

~5%

EAT

Exercise activity thermogenesis

Calories burned in formal exercise. Shockingly, for most people this is the smallest piece of the pie.

Even the best calculation is still just a calculation.

A formula takes a few metrics into account. In reality, countless things change how much you burn in a day: sleep, hormone health, what you eat and more.
