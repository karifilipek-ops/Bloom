The breakdown

Total Daily Energy Expenditure.

Where you actually burn calories in a day. It's made of four parts.

01 — ~70%

Basal metabolic rate

The calories your body burns at complete rest: keeping your heart beating, lungs breathing, brain thinking and temperature regulated, 24/7. It's about 70% of what you burn in a day, and the part certain metabolic conditions can suppress (hypothyroidism can lower it by 5–15%).

02 — Varies

Non-exercise activity

Calories from any movement that isn't formal exercise: steps, fidgeting, standing instead of sitting. Depending on your lifestyle it's the smallest chunk or the second biggest, and it's one of the best levers for burning more.

03 — 10–15%

Thermic effect of food

Calories burned digesting food. Protein costs 20–30% of its calories to digest, so 100 calories of protein burns 20–30 just breaking it down. Fat costs only 0–3%.

04 — ~5%

Exercise

Calories burned in formal exercise. Shockingly, for most people this is the smallest piece of the pie.

Even the best calculation is still just a calculation.

A formula takes a few metrics into account. In reality, countless things change how much you burn in a day: sleep, hormone health, what you eat and more.
