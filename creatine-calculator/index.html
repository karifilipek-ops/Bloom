How this is calculated

Why not just 5 g? The classic 3–5 g a day was set for an average-sized person. Research now doses creatine by body weight, about 0.1 g per kg a day (Antonio et al., 2021), with studies using 0.03–0.14 g/kg. So bigger and more active people need more.

Your range runs from about 0.07 to 0.1 g per kg of body weight (never under 5 g). More weekly training moves your sweet spot toward the top.

Adjustments: +2 g if you eat mostly plant-based (lower starting stores). If you’re 50+, the low end goes up. For brain benefits the range goes up about 3–5 g: the brain takes up creatine slowly, and studies that raised brain levels used higher doses (Candow et al., 2023). Capped at 20 g a day.

Safety: creatine is one of the most-studied supplements and is well tolerated in healthy people, including long term.
